# Notes for slice C5 (ADR-0078, CMMC SSP, POA&M, evidence, OSCAL export, SPRS range), for the integrator

## Changelog lines

- Compliance (ADR-0078 C5): a CMMC assessment workspace in the organization's Compliance tab. Assessments move from draft to in review to approved to archived; approval freezes the record and a new draft is made from it. Scope is recorded per computer; each requirement shows its objectives, a statement, who is responsible, and the evidence attached; an attestation needs a fresh step-up and expires after a year. The SPRS score is shown as a range (unassessed requirements counted as failing, then as passing) and as one number only when none is unassessed; a plan of action board drafts items for requirements not met and applies the 32 CFR 170.21 conditions; the system security plan is written from EasyRMM's own template. Exports: OSCAL 1.1.2 system security plan and plan of action, CSV, PDF and a ZIP with each file's SHA-256. Nothing is posted to SPRS.
- Evidence is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM under a per-organization key wrapped by the server key), limited to 64 MiB, its SHA-256 is checked on every download, uploads and downloads are in the audit log, and it is never deleted, only replaced.
- Fixed: the SPRS weight of 3.7.5 is 5 (the weights now total 313 and were checked against the DoD methodology, version 1.2.1).

## Privacy notes

- Evidence may hold controlled unclassified information. It is stored encrypted, readable only with `compliance.view` in that organization, and each upload and download is audited with the evidence id and hash (never the content). Exports include the organization's name, computer names in scope and the statements people wrote; they leave the platform only when a person downloads them, and the ZIP lists evidence by hash only.
- The audit log holds statement hashes (not statements) for edits.
