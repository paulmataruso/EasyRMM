## Changelog lines

* Notification rules: an organization's administrators (and super
  administrators, for the platform's) choose which device alerts and notices
  are sent, filtered by organization (platform rules), site, device group,
  kind of device, severity and condition, when (at once or after a wait,
  reminders, resolutions, acknowledgements to webhooks), and to which
  channels (ADR-0053 slice 4).
* The alerts' journal is written by the database; the planner matches it
  against the rules every 15 seconds, so that nothing is lost or queued twice
  across restarts.
* Incidents: one ID from opening to resolution, across a policy change.
* Maintenance windows per site (weekly or once, daylight saving handled);
  site outages ("Site X: all 40 devices offline"); digests for people;
  the fleet-wide hold when most devices go silent at once; a device's kind
  (the agent reports `role`; someone with `monitoring.manage` can set it).

## Privacy

Rules hold no personal data. Alert incidents keep the device, site and
organization names as they were when the alert opened, and the label of whoever
acknowledged (an email address), until 7 days after resolution; deliveries keep
what slice 3 says.

## Dev scope

`devscope.Allowed` is checked when an alert opens (an organization outside the
scope gets no incident, so nothing is ever sent), for notices, in the due pass,
and in the sender (slice 3).
