# Notes for the integrator: slice C2 (ADR-0078, results and the fix queue)

## Changelog lines

- Compliance fix queue (ADR-0078, slice C2): each control that does not meet on a computer becomes a queue item with a risk tier (safe, can break a
  workflow, can cut access). A fix is made under a recorded agreement (the warning's id and hash, the person, the time, the preconditions); a fix that can cut access
  needs the computer's name typed and a step-up, one computer at a time; there is no "fix all" above the safe tier. Fixes are made through the existing Windows hardening apply and
  undo, one setting at a time, then the control is checked again. Undo, history, approval by a second person (optional per tier), and setting an item aside as an accepted
  risk or a deferral are in.
- Results screens: the organization's Compliance tab (counts, requirements by framework, queue, scan, approval setting), a result card on the site's Overview, a Compliance tab on a
  computer (controls, its requirements by framework, its items) and a "Compliance: failing" filter on the Devices list.
- Hardening results of single-setting changes keep each setting's before and after value (`hardening_setting_results`).

## Privacy notes

- A queue item holds a computer's configuration value (the setting's current and wanted value) per organization, behind row-level security and `compliance.view`. An agreement holds the
  user id, the time, the warning's id and hash, and, for a change that can cut access, the computer's name as typed. Both are kept like the scan history; neither holds personal data beyond the user id.
- Audit events `compliance.fix.*` carry the control, its version, the tier and the agreement id.
